How to read the results

“Before” and “after” compare the same tax scenario without and with cashback. They do not automatically mean before and after the reform. The burden is the ratio of weighted tax totals to weighted income totals, not the average of individual burdens.

The first decile is the lowest-income 10% of the weighted population defined in the input. The Kakwani index is computed on the original observations and uses the same reference income. A positive index indicates progressivity; a negative index, regressivity. A zero aggregate tax leaves the index undefined.

The basic food basket must be built into the item-level tax calculation. Different basket scenarios require new simulations. Eligibility, formality of consumption, prices, take-up and refund rules must be documented in the empirical work. This dashboard does not estimate causal effects or confidence intervals.
