Dissertation

Static microsimulation of Brazil’s consumption-tax reform (Constitutional Amendment 132/2023; Complementary Law 214/2025) using the 2017–2018 Household Budget Survey (POF/IBGE). Simulates the CBS/IBS burden by income decile at a 26.5% reference rate and measures the regressivity that remains after the cashback to families with per capita income up to half a minimum wage, treating the formality of each consumption channel as the condition for the refund to reach households.
